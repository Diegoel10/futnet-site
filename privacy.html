1. Information We Collect

When you register or sign in via email or social providers (like Google), FutNet collects basic profile information such as your name, email address, profile picture avatar, date of birth, and preferred position to facilitate match organization.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Manage your account and authentication securely via Firebase.
  • Display your profile on game rosters, leaderboards, and team line-up tools.
  • Allow other users to invite you to matches or connect within the app directory.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Profile details like your name and avatar are visible to other logged-in users solely for soccer match coordination purposes.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

FutNet utilizes secure session tokens and local browser storage to keep you logged in and preserve your match preferences.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding your data or wish to delete your account, you can reach out directly through the platform support channels.
